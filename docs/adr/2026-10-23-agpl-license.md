# nudge is released under AGPL-3.0

nudge is a personal, open-source, self-hosted project. We choose AGPL-3.0 over MIT or Apache-2.0: self-hosting stays completely free, but anyone offering nudge as a hosted service must publish their changes. This leaves the door open to a hosted offering from the author without letting a third party turn it into a closed SaaS.

## Consequences

Changing the license after external contributions arrive requires the agreement of every contributor, or a CLA put in place before the first contribution.
